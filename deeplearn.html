What Is Deep Learning?

Deep learning is a way for computers to learn from examples. Instead of having someone explain every single thing to them, computers can look at lots of information and start noticing patterns. This helps them do things like recognize faces, understand voices, and identify pictures.

We use deep learning in more places than we might think. It can help voice assistants understand what we say, and it can help apps pick videos we might want to watch. I think it is cool how computers can use information to get better at certain tasks. It will be interesting to see what else this technology can do in the future.
