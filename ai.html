What Is AI?

AI is short for artificial intelligence. It might sound complicated, but we actually see it in a lot of things we use every day. For example, some phones can answer questions, apps can suggest things we might like, and computers can help us find information faster.

I think AI is pretty interesting because it can help people with everyday tasks and make some things easier. It is not perfect, though, and it can still make mistakes. As technology keeps changing, I think we will see AI become an even bigger part of our lives.
